# PlaceAI opportunity presentation

Uses the supplied Pod AI screenshots as a structural reference only: banner, overlapping role/company identity, facts, description, eligibility and selection process, with registration and company information in a separate side panel. Uses PlaceAI's existing plum palette and controls. Mobile keeps the primary application action below the role summary.

Real opportunity data comes from the student's authorized job, recruiter profile and available institution drive. No invented company size, salary, eligibility, contact identity or selection stages. The fallback company mark is an initial-based avatar. Existing Apply and server validation remain authoritative.

TechNova is explicitly fictional. Its separate preview is returned by an authenticated read-only endpoint and creates no job or application records. The pre-existing TechNova assessment can still use its existing application/assessment workflow.

## Final image generation prompt
Create an original photographic website banner for PlaceAI campus career opportunities, landscape ultra-wide composition intended to crop to about 5:1. High-end editorial architectural photograph of a contemporary Indian technology workplace: warm ivory stone, smoked glass, walnut desks, subtle muted plum upholstery, indoor plants and a softly visible modern city skyline through large windows. A few small distant adult professionals in natural candid positions, no close-up faces. Premium understated corporate atmosphere with daylight, realistic materials, clean composition, no text, no numbers, no logos, no interface elements. Keep central band visually interesting, objects away from frame edges, suitable for an accessible dark gradient overlay. Original fictional location, do not reproduce the supplied competitor screenshot.
