01 · Assessment blueprint

Prepare your full placement simulation

PlaceAI automatically builds the mock from the opportunity, role requirements and a standardized campus-placement assessment blueprint.

50-question minimum
Assessment configuration Standardized Full Mock Students cannot reduce question count, remove sections, lower difficulty or switch to a shorter round.
Assessment rules
  • Questions are delivered one at a time and cannot be revisited after submission.
  • Question and option text is rendered to a protected canvas layer with candidate watermarking.
  • Copy, paste, context-menu and common clipboard shortcuts are blocked during the assessment.
  • Leaving full-screen or switching away pauses the test and creates an integrity event.
  • Integrity events are review signals, not automatic allegations of misconduct.
