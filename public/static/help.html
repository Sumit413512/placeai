Learning request workflow

  1. Requested: your request is saved immediately and remains in Your video courses or Your notebooks.

  2. Processing: PlaceAI builds the complete module structure and prepares every required lesson or section. You can leave and return later.

  3. Approval: automated content, media and audio-visual checks complete first; a platform administrator then reviews the package.

  4. Ready to begin: an in-app update appears and the learning button becomes available. Your progress is saved as you study.

If a generated lesson is incorrect, an administrator can request correction for that individual video. PlaceAI regenerates it from the saved review findings and repeats all publication checks before the course can be approved.

Account and access help

Use password reset from the sign-in dialog if you cannot access your account. Privileged roles must use the approved role and verified email linked to their workspace. Do not create a second account to bypass an access review.

Placement workflow help

Applications and campus opportunities are scoped to your account and institution. Profile fields marked as institution-verified may require placement-office approval. Documents remain private until you explicitly submit them to an authorized institution.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my course still processing?

A complete course can contain many 5–10 minute lessons. Provider capacity, rendering and independent quality checks may extend preparation time. Completed parts remain saved.

Can a requested course disappear?

No normal refresh or sign-out should remove it. It remains attached to your account until you deliberately choose Remove course. If a processing fault occurs, the request is preserved for repair.

Why can’t I start a course immediately?

The complete course must be generated, pass content and media checks, and receive human approval. This prevents an incomplete or unreviewed lesson from being presented as ready.

How do captions and playback speed work?

Captions start off. Use the video player's menu to turn captions on or change playback speed when required.

How do I delete learning content?

Notes move to Deleted notes and can be restored. Removing a video course removes it and its private viewing progress from your learning library; an approved shared source course remains available for other learners.
