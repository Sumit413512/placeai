These prices reflect the current independent-student entitlement catalog in the production application. Online checkout remains disabled until merchant onboarding is complete.

Currency: INR

Independent Student — ₹299 / 30 days

For independent students using PlaceAI preparation features. Eligible accounts receive a 3-day preparation trial, after which ₹299 activates 30 days of premium preparation access. This checkout does not auto-renew; a new verified payment is required for another 30-day period. University-linked students use institution-sponsored access instead.

AI Career Roadmap — ₹20 one time

A one-time feature unlock for eligible independent students. The Career Roadmap is not included in the free trial. It is available through active paid-plan access or an explicit one-time entitlement according to the production access rules.

University and institutional access

Institutional packages are provided through an authorized PlaceAI access and commercial process rather than the independent-student checkout. Institutions can use Request access to start that process.
