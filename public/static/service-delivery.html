Service Delivery Policy

PlaceAI provides software and digital access. No physical product is shipped for the student plans or feature unlocks described on this website.

Website: www.placeai.in

1. Free trial access

Eligible independent students receive the current 3-day preparation trial through their PlaceAI account. Trial timing is determined by the production entitlement rules and begins from the account timing recorded by PlaceAI.

2. Independent Student subscription

When live payment collection is activated, paid subscription access will be granted only after the selected payment provider reports a successful, verified payment or subscription event. The entitlement is designed to appear on the same PlaceAI student account shortly after successful verification. A pending, failed or unverified payment does not count as delivered paid access.

3. AI Career Roadmap one-time unlock

When live checkout is activated, the ₹20 Career Roadmap entitlement will be unlocked only after PlaceAI verifies the successful provider payment for that student account. If a captured payment is verified but the feature is not delivered, PlaceAI will attempt entitlement restoration and apply the published Refund & Cancellation Policy if delivery cannot be restored.

4. Institution-sponsored access

University and institution access is provisioned through the authorized PlaceAI onboarding and commercial process. It is not delivered through the independent-student checkout and may depend on institution approval, account provisioning and the applicable agreement.

5. No physical shipping

PlaceAI is a digital software service. There are no shipping charges, courier deliveries or physical fulfilment timelines for the student subscription or Career Roadmap unlock.

6. Checkout status

Live online checkout is currently disabled while merchant onboarding, KYC, settlement verification and billing-support activation are pending. This page describes digital fulfilment rules for future verified transactions; it is not an instruction to transfer money outside an official PlaceAI checkout.
