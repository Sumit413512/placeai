About PlaceAI

Campus placement operations, connected end to end.

PlaceAI is software for higher-education placement teams, recruiters and students. It brings controlled access, placement workflows, student preparation, interview practice and reporting into one role-aware platform.

Website: www.placeai.in

What PlaceAI does

PlaceAI supports student readiness, approved opportunities, placement drives, recruiter pipelines, interviews, offers, reporting and AI-assisted preparation. Production records are created by authenticated users and remain subject to role and institution boundaries.

Who PlaceAI serves

The platform is designed for institutions running campus placement operations, recruiters participating in authorized hiring workflows, university-linked students and independent students using preparation features.

AI and human review

AI-assisted outputs are intended for preparation or authorized decision support. They can be incomplete or inaccurate and do not replace lawful human review. PlaceAI does not guarantee employment, placement outcomes or candidate suitability.

Commercial availability

PlaceAI includes institution-sponsored access and independent-student preparation plans. Privacy and security

PlaceAI uses authenticated role-based access, institution scoping, secured production storage and privacy-aware AI processing controls. See the Privacy Policy, Terms of Use and Acceptable Use Policy for more information.
