PlaceAI pricing

PlaceAI currently focuses on institutional partnerships. Students use one shared workspace, whether they register directly or through a college. Online checkout remains disabled.

Currency: INR

Shared student workspace

Learning Hub, interview preparation, readiness, resume tools and the student placement workflow are available through the same student workspace. No individual subscription or three-day preparation cutoff is applied during the current rollout.

AI Career Roadmap included

Career Roadmap is included with current student access. Students do not need to buy a separate feature unlock.

University and institutional access

Institutional packages are agreed through the authorized PlaceAI onboarding process based on college requirements. Institutions can use Request access to start that process.

Checkout status

Online payment collection is not active yet. Institutional quotations are provided directly; this page is not a request to transfer money to a personal account. PlaceAI will enable checkout only after a verified payment provider, merchant/KYC approval, settlement account and billing-support channel are in place.

Future paid transactions will be subject to the Refund & Cancellation Policy, Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
