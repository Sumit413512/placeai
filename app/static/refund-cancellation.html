Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy describes the rules PlaceAI intends to apply when live online payments are activated. Checkout is currently disabled while merchant onboarding and settlement verification are pending.

Effective for future live checkout: 8 October 2026
No online PlaceAI payment should be treated as available until the checkout page itself is enabled and identifies the active payment provider. This policy does not create a payment method where checkout remains disabled.

1. Independent-student subscription

The current independent-student product catalog is ₹299 per month. Once recurring checkout is activated, a student may cancel future renewal before the next billing event. Cancellation stops future renewal; access may continue through the already-paid subscription period unless applicable law or the checkout terms require otherwise.

2. AI Career Roadmap one-time unlock

The current AI Career Roadmap unlock is ₹20 one time. If a verified payment is captured but the entitlement is not delivered, PlaceAI will first attempt to restore the purchased access. If delivery cannot be restored within a reasonable support process, the payment may be refunded to the original payment method, subject to applicable law and payment-provider rules.

3. Duplicate or incorrect charges

Verified duplicate captures, incorrect amounts, or charges created by a PlaceAI billing defect are eligible for correction or refund after payment-provider verification. Users should not disclose card numbers, UPI PINs, OTPs, passwords or other authentication secrets when requesting support.

4. Successfully delivered digital access

Except where required by applicable law, refunds are generally not provided solely because a user changes their mind after the paid digital service or feature has been successfully unlocked and materially used. This does not limit statutory consumer rights or remedies for non-delivery, duplicate charging or service defects.

5. Refund processing

Once a refund is approved and live payments are active, PlaceAI will initiate it through the original payment provider as promptly as reasonably practicable. Final credit timing depends on the payment provider, banking network and the user's bank or payment instrument.

6. How to request billing help

Use the billing-support contact published on the Contact page once live checkout is enabled. Until a verified billing-support channel and merchant account are published, PlaceAI checkout remains disabled and no user should be asked to pay through an unofficial personal transfer.

7. Policy changes

PlaceAI may update this policy when its payment provider, subscription model or legal obligations change. The version presented with live checkout will govern the transaction, subject to applicable law.
