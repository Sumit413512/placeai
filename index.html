Placement operations from readiness to offer.

The application is built around institution-controlled placement workflows rather than disconnected forms and spreadsheets.

Institution command centre

Manage batches, eligibility, drives, announcements, recruiter access, placement policies, reports and outcomes.

Student workspace

Profile readiness, documents, opportunities, applications, mock interviews, attendance, notifications and offer tracking.

Recruiter workflow

Verified recruiter profiles, campus jobs, applicant pipeline, interview coordination, communication and placement intelligence.

Advanced drive pipelines

Configurable screening, assessment, technical, HR, offer and joined stages with institution-scoped references.

Security controls

Argon2 passwords, rotating refresh sessions, replay prevention, throttling, hardened uploads and cross-tenant validation.

AI with guardrails

Optional Gemini-assisted resume, matching and interview workflows that fail explicitly when AI is unavailable.

Clear deployment separation.

The public URL and production application are intentionally different surfaces so static hosting never masquerades as a working backend.
